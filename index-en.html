Operational challenges

Is your Brazilian operation facing these problems?

Symptoms appear in different places, but their causes are often connected.

High transportation cost

Freight rates, low consolidation, weak carrier strategy and service policies reduce margin.

Unreliable inventory

System balances do not match physical stock, creating rework and customer risk.

Warehouse bottlenecks

Receiving, storage, picking and shipping cannot keep pace with demand.

WMS or ERP underperformance

Technology is implemented without stable processes, governance or reliable data.

Missing indicators

Leaders cannot see productivity, cost, backlog, accuracy or service level clearly.

Global standards, weak local execution

Corporate procedures need practical adaptation to the Brazilian operation.
